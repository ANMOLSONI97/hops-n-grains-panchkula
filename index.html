The story

Rooted in
Panchkula.

Hops n Grains opened in Sector 9, Panchkula in 2010. Since then it has grown into The Great Bear in Chandigarh and a second Hops n Grains in Mohali, carrying the same recipe: thoughtfully prepared food, genuine hospitality and a welcoming space.

Guests became regulars and friends. Reviewers consistently single out the craft beer, the ambience and the service.
